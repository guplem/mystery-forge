# Mystery Forge

Make your own printable mystery game with an AI agent. You choose the players, the length, the difficulty, the language, and the look. The agent writes a story, builds and checks the puzzles, and gives you PDFs to print plus a companion page for your phone or computer.

> Work in progress. The first complete version is being built now.

## What you get

- **A game to print**: envelopes of clues, letters, maps, codes, and puzzles, with a cover sheet that hides each envelope until the game tells you to open it.
- **A manual**: how to print, how to set up, and how to play.
- **Hints and solutions**: separate files, with warnings, so nobody sees a spoiler by accident.
- **A companion page**: one offline HTML file that checks your answers, gives one hint at a time, and keeps a timer.
